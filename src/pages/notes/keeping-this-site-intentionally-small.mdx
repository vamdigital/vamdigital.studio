There is always another feature a personal site could have: more animation, more content types, more integrations, and more ways to organise everything.

I am choosing a smaller starting point. A handful of clear pages, writing in Markdown, and just enough motion to make navigation feel connected.

The constraint is useful. It keeps attention on the work and the words rather than on the machinery surrounding them.

Small does not mean unfinished. It means every element has to earn its place.
